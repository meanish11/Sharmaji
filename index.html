Our Heritage ❖

Not just a sweet snack.
A piece of home you can taste.

In Bihar, Jharkhand, and Eastern Uttar Pradesh, Thekua is sacred. It is the pious prasad of Chhath Puja, the quintessential teatime treasure, and the affectionate pouch mothers secretly pack into bags whenever their children leave for college, job postings, or distant cities.

At Sharma Ji Thekua, we treat this heritage with the dignity it deserves. No machines, no commercial palm oils, no synthetic stabilizers. We hand-knead the flour with warm melted desi ghee (moyen), press each piece into seasoned wooden moulds (thekua sancha), and gently slow-fry them in pure ghee over a low kadhai flame to achieve that melt-in-the-mouth, crumbly golden crunch.

Every pouch brings the comforting nostalgia of home kitchens directly to your doorstep — wherever in India life has taken you.

Traditional Preparation ❖

Four steps. Zero shortcuts.

How authentic Bihari thekua has been crafted for generations — slow, disciplined, and full of soul.

01 सानना (Sanna)

Gentle Kneading

Wholesome flour is blended with pure melted desi ghee (moyen), warm sugar-infused water, and fragrant crushed green cardamom (elaichi). Hand-rubbed until it achieves a crumbly, aromatic texture that holds together when gently pressed in the palm.

Desi Ghee Moyen · Hand Folded
02 गढ़ना (Ghadna)

Heirloom Moulding

Small balls of dough are pressed into seasoned wooden moulds (thekua sancha). This leaves the iconic traditional leaf and floral relief that has been the sacred signature of Bihari household kitchens for centuries.

Wooden Sancha · Hand Pressed
03 तलना (Talna)

Unhurried Slow-Fry

Lowered into hot, pure desi ghee in a heavy kadhai over a gentle, steady flame. Never rushed. The low flame ensures the thekua cooks completely through to the core, yielding a crisp crust with a tender, aromatic bite.

Deep Kadhai · Low Gentle Flame
04 सुरक्षा (Suraksha)

Cooling & Packaging

Allowed to cool completely at room temperature so the outer texture achieves its characteristic crunch. Sealed within hours into food-grade, airtight ziplock stand-up pouches to lock in that fresh desi ghee aroma.

Freshness Sealed · Dispatched Weekly

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ingredients, packing, shelf-life, and delivery.

What ingredients are used in Sharma Ji Thekua? +

We use only wholesome, traditional kitchen ingredients: premium Refined Wheat Flour (Maida), pure Desi Cow Ghee, Sugar, Purified Water, and authentic flavour infusions (Fresh Cardamom for Elaichi, Rose Petals for Gulab, and Rich Cocoa for Chocolate). We strictly do NOT use palm oil, dalda, artificial colours, or preservatives.

How long does the thekua stay fresh (shelf life)? +

Because our thekua is fried in pure desi ghee and moisture is completely baked out during the slow-frying process, it has a natural shelf life of 45 days from the packaging date. Keep it sealed in our ziplock pouch or transfer it to an airtight stainless steel or glass container at room temperature away from direct heat and moisture.

Each pack is first hermetically sealed in a food-grade stand-up pouch, then cushioned inside a sturdy, rigid corrugated outer box. We ship across India through trusted express courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, DTDC).
